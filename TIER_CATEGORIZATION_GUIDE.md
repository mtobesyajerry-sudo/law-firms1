# Bank and Financial Institution Tier Categorization System

## Overview

The AML Risk Assessment System implements a FATF-consistent, risk-based three-tier approach designed specifically for banks and financial institutions in Tanzania. This approach is proportionate, aligned with Bank of Tanzania guidelines, and defensible to supervisors.

## Core Design Principle

**All financial institutions must demonstrate technical compliance with AML/CFT/CPF requirements.**

**Financial institutions with higher inherent risk, larger scale, or more complex operations must demonstrate effectiveness in depth.**

This principle ensures:
- FATF consistency
- Proportionality based on institutional risk
- Fairness across different institution types and sizes
- Defensibility to Bank of Tanzania and FIU

## Three-Tier Structure

### Tier 1: Small / Limited-Service Financial Institutions

**Question Count:** ~50 questions

**Coverage:**
- **Technical Compliance:** Core TC requirements
- **Effectiveness:** Basic operational effectiveness

**Purpose:** Establish baseline AML/CFT compliance

**Who this is for:**
- Small microfinance institutions
- Small SACCOs/Credit Unions
- Community banks with limited geographic scope
- Institutions with 1-10 employees
- Limited product offerings (basic savings and loans only)
- Domestic-only operations
- No cross-border transactions
- No correspondent banking relationships

**Automatic Assignment:**
- 1-10 employees AND
- Under 100 Million TZS annual transaction volume AND
- Single branch or service point AND
- No high-risk products (FX, remittances, private banking)

**What they must demonstrate:**

**Governance & Policies:**
- AML/CFT/CPF policy manual exists and is board-approved
- Designated AML Compliance Officer appointed
- Basic senior management oversight

**Customer Due Diligence:**
- CDD procedures for account opening
- ID verification processes
- Basic beneficial ownership identification

**Transaction Monitoring:**
- Manual transaction monitoring procedures
- Awareness of suspicious activity indicators
- Process for escalating suspicions

**Reporting & Record Keeping:**
- STR reporting procedures
- 5-year record retention policy
- Basic record accessibility

**Training:**
- Annual AML/CFT training for all staff
- Training records maintained

**Effectiveness Questions (Basic & Diagnostic):**
- One example of CDD preventing account opening
- How suspicious transactions are escalated
- Evidence of at least one STR filed (or documented reasons why not)

---

### Tier 2: Medium-Scale Financial Institutions

**Question Count:** ~75 questions

**Coverage:**
- **Technical Compliance:** Full TC requirements
- **Effectiveness:** Operational effectiveness testing

**Purpose:** Verify that AML/CFT controls operate effectively

**Who this is for:**
- Microfinance banks
- Medium-sized commercial banks (regional presence)
- Larger SACCOs/Credit Unions
- Insurance companies
- Payment service providers
- Institutions with 11-200 employees
- Multiple branches or service points
- Moderate product range (savings, loans, basic FX)
- Some cross-border transactions
- Limited correspondent banking

**Automatic Assignment:**
- 11-200 employees OR
- 100 Million - 1 Billion TZS annual transaction volume OR
- Multiple branches (2-10 locations) OR
- Institution categories: Microfinance Bank, Community Bank, Credit Union/SACCOS, Insurance Company, Payment Service Provider

**What they must demonstrate:**

All Tier 1 requirements PLUS:

**Enhanced Governance:**
- Board-level AML oversight committee
- Regular compliance reporting to Board
- Adequate compliance resources and budget

**Risk Assessment:**
- Documented institutional risk assessment
- Customer risk rating system
- Annual risk assessment updates

**Enhanced CDD:**
- Enhanced Due Diligence for high-risk customers
- PEP screening procedures
- Source of funds verification for high-risk accounts

**Transaction Monitoring:**
- Documented monitoring thresholds and scenarios
- Alert investigation and documentation
- Transaction profile analysis for high-risk customers

**Sanctions Screening:**
- Screening against UN, US, EU sanctions lists
- Ongoing sanctions screening procedures

**Internal Controls:**
- Independent compliance testing or sampling
- Management information systems for AML metrics
- Audit trail for all CDD and monitoring activities

**Effectiveness Questions (Operational & Outcome-Oriented):**
- Examples of risk assessment influencing controls
- Evidence of CDD quality (rejected applications, account closures)
- Timeliness of STR filing (from detection to submission)
- Training effectiveness (staff knowledge testing)
- Transaction monitoring generating actionable alerts
- PEP identification and enhanced monitoring

**What NOT to ask Tier 2:**
- Detailed correspondent banking due diligence
- Cross-border wire transfer screening systems
- Advanced analytics or AI-based monitoring
- Extensive international sanctions compliance programs

---

### Tier 3: Large / High-Risk Financial Institutions

**Question Count:** ~100 questions

**Coverage:**
- **Technical Compliance:** Full TC with supporting evidence
- **Effectiveness:** Deep behavioral effectiveness testing

**Purpose:** Test outcomes, risk mitigation, and control effectiveness under pressure

**Who this is for:**
- Commercial banks (full-service)
- Investment banks
- Large regional or national banks
- Bureaus de change
- Money transfer services
- Institutions with correspondent banking relationships
- Institutions with 201+ employees
- Full product suite (including private banking, trade finance)
- Significant cross-border operations
- Multiple international correspondent relationships
- Non-face-to-face account opening

**Automatic Assignment (Category-Based):**
- Commercial Bank (always Tier 3)
- Investment Bank (always Tier 3)
- Bureau de Change (always Tier 3)
- Money Transfer Service (always Tier 3)

**OR (Size/Complexity-Based):**
- 201+ employees OR
- Over 1 Billion TZS annual transaction volume OR
- 11+ branches nationwide OR
- Correspondent banking relationships OR
- Cross-border operations exceeding 20% of business

**What they must demonstrate:**

All Tier 1 and Tier 2 requirements PLUS:

**Advanced Governance:**
- Three lines of defense model documented
- Board AML expertise or external advisors
- MLRO independence from business pressures
- Compliance staff adequate for institutional complexity

**Comprehensive Risk Assessment:**
- Detailed institutional risk assessment covering all FATF risk factors
- Product-specific risk assessments
- Geographic risk assessment (country risk ratings)
- Regular scenario analysis and stress testing

**Advanced CDD:**
- Enhanced Due Diligence procedures fully documented
- Source of wealth verification for PEPs and high-risk customers
- Ongoing monitoring and periodic review schedules
- Beneficial ownership verification through independent sources

**Sophisticated Transaction Monitoring:**
- Automated transaction monitoring system
- Regularly updated monitoring rules and scenarios
- Alert investigation quality assurance
- Cross-border wire transfer screening (SWIFT analysis)

**Correspondent Banking:**
- Enhanced due diligence on all correspondent banks
- Assessment of correspondent AML controls
- Ongoing monitoring of correspondent relationships
- Clear prohibition on shell bank relationships

**Sanctions & PEP Screening:**
- Real-time screening against comprehensive sanctions lists
- Automated PEP screening at onboarding and periodically
- Name-matching technology with false positive management
- Adverse media screening for high-risk customers

**Independent Testing:**
- Annual independent AML audit
- Regular compliance sampling and testing
- Third-party assessments or certifications
- Gap analysis and remediation tracking

**Regulatory Cooperation:**
- Proactive engagement with Bank of Tanzania
- Timely response to supervisory requests
- FIU feedback incorporation into controls

**Effectiveness Questions (Deep & Behavioral):**
- Senior management challenge of compliance decisions
- MLRO independence in high-pressure situations
- Real case studies of suspicious activity detection and STR filing
- Examples of relationship termination due to ML/TF concerns
- Evidence of transaction monitoring preventing layering
- Correspondent banking due diligence preventing shell bank access
- Learning from past weaknesses or near-misses
- De-risking decisions and documentation
- Customer exit strategy for unmanageable risks

**What Tier 3 must accept:**
- Higher supervisory expectations
- More detailed narrative responses required
- Greater scrutiny of "no issues identified" responses
- Evidence-based demonstration of effectiveness
- Lower tolerance for control gaps

## Financial Institution Category Classification

### Tier 3 Institutions (Automatic - High Systemic Risk)

These institution types ALWAYS default to Tier 3, regardless of size, due to inherent high risk:

1. **Commercial Banks**
   - Full banking license
   - Deposit-taking and lending
   - High transaction volumes
   - Systemic importance
   - Cross-border operations
   - Correspondent banking relationships

2. **Investment Banks**
   - Securities trading and underwriting
   - Corporate finance advisory
   - Complex financial products
   - High-value transactions
   - Cross-border investment flows

3. **Bureaus de Change**
   - Foreign exchange trading
   - Cash-intensive operations
   - High anonymity risk
   - Cross-border currency flows
   - Rapid transaction execution

4. **Money Transfer Services**
   - Domestic and international remittances
   - High transaction volumes
   - Agent network complexity
   - Cross-border by nature
   - Layering opportunities

### Tier 2 Institutions (Default - Medium Risk)

Can be Tier 1 if very small, Tier 3 if very large:

5. **Microfinance Banks**
   - Small-scale lending focus
   - Limited product range
   - Growing regulatory oversight
   - Increasing complexity

6. **Community Banks**
   - Localized operations
   - Community development focus
   - Limited geographic scope
   - Growing customer base

7. **Credit Unions / SACCOS**
   - Member-owned cooperatives
   - Savings and credit services
   - Community-based
   - Limited product offerings

8. **Insurance Companies**
   - Premium collection
   - Claims processing
   - Investment management
   - Some cash handling

9. **Payment Service Providers**
   - Electronic payment processing
   - Mobile money services
   - Digital wallets
   - Rapid technological change

10. **Other Financial Institutions**
    - Various specialized services
    - Risk-based categorization
    - Assessed case-by-case

## Tier Determination Logic

The system uses a **hierarchical decision flow** considering three factors:

```
Factor 1: Institution Type (high-risk types → automatic Tier 3)
Factor 2: Transaction Volume & Scale (systemic importance)
Factor 3: Operational Complexity (branches, products, geography)
```

### Decision Logic (Applied in Order)

**Step 1: Check if High-Risk Institution Type**
```
IF institution type is ANY of:
   - Commercial Bank
   - Investment Bank
   - Bureau de Change
   - Money Transfer Service
→ ASSIGN TIER 3 (regardless of size or volume)
```

**Step 2: Check if Very Small/Limited Service**
```
ELSE IF employees = '1-10'
   AND transaction volume < 100M TZS annually
   AND single branch/location
   AND basic products only (no FX, remittances, private banking)
→ ASSIGN TIER 1
```

**Step 3: Check if Large/Complex**
```
ELSE IF employees = '201+'
   OR transaction volume > 1B TZS annually
   OR branches >= 11
   OR correspondent banking relationships exist
   OR cross-border operations > 20% of business
→ ASSIGN TIER 3
```

**Step 4: Default Medium-Size/Complexity**
```
ELSE
→ ASSIGN TIER 2
```

## Tier Assignment Examples

### Example 1: Small SACCOS
- **Employees:** 8
- **Transaction Volume:** 50 Million TZS annually
- **Branches:** 1
- **Products:** Basic savings and small loans
- **Assigned Tier:** 1
- **Rationale:** Small scale, limited products, single location, low complexity

### Example 2: Microfinance Bank
- **Employees:** 45
- **Transaction Volume:** 500 Million TZS annually
- **Branches:** 5 regional offices
- **Products:** Savings, loans, basic mobile money
- **Assigned Tier:** 2
- **Rationale:** Medium scale, moderate complexity, multi-branch operations

### Example 3: Commercial Bank
- **Employees:** 350
- **Transaction Volume:** 50 Billion TZS annually
- **Branches:** 25 nationwide
- **Products:** Full suite including FX, trade finance, private banking
- **Assigned Tier:** 3
- **Rationale:** Automatic Tier 3 due to institution type (Commercial Bank)

### Example 4: Small Bureau de Change
- **Employees:** 5
- **Transaction Volume:** 80 Million TZS annually
- **Branches:** 1 location
- **Products:** Foreign exchange only
- **Assigned Tier:** 3
- **Rationale:** Automatic Tier 3 due to institution type (high-risk category)

### Example 5: Payment Service Provider
- **Employees:** 120
- **Transaction Volume:** 2 Billion TZS annually
- **Branches:** Mobile/digital only
- **Products:** Mobile money, bill payments, merchant services
- **Assigned Tier:** 3
- **Rationale:** High transaction volume triggers Tier 3

### Example 6: Insurance Company
- **Employees:** 60
- **Transaction Volume:** 400 Million TZS annually
- **Branches:** 3 regional offices
- **Products:** Life and general insurance
- **Assigned Tier:** 2
- **Rationale:** Medium-risk institution type, moderate scale

## Question Distribution by Module

| Tier | Module 1 (Inherent Risk) | Module 2 (Compliance) | Module 3 (Effectiveness) | Total |
|------|--------------------------|----------------------|--------------------------|-------|
| 1    | 20 questions             | 25 questions         | 5 questions              | 50    |
| 2    | 30 questions             | 35 questions         | 10 questions             | 75    |
| 3    | 42 questions             | 45 questions         | 13 questions             | 100   |

### Module Breakdown by Tier

**Module 1: Inherent Risk Assessment**
- **Tier 1:** Basic product, customer, and geographic risk
- **Tier 2:** Adds delivery channel risk and transaction complexity
- **Tier 3:** Adds volume/scale analysis, correspondent banking risk, cross-border complexity

**Module 2: AML/CFT Compliance Controls**
- **Tier 1:** Core governance, basic CDD, manual monitoring, STR procedures, record keeping, training
- **Tier 2:** Adds risk assessment, enhanced CDD, sanctions screening, internal testing, audit
- **Tier 3:** Adds correspondent banking DD, automated systems, three lines of defense, regulatory engagement

**Module 3: Operational Effectiveness**
- **Tier 1:** Basic evidence of controls functioning
- **Tier 2:** Evidence of control effectiveness and outcomes
- **Tier 3:** Deep testing of effectiveness under pressure, case studies, behavioral testing

## Benefits of This Bank-Specific Approach

### 1. Risk-Based Proportionality
- Small institutions not overwhelmed with irrelevant requirements
- High-risk institutions receive appropriate scrutiny
- Resources allocated based on ML/TF/PF risk

### 2. Regulatory Alignment
- Follows Bank of Tanzania risk-based supervision principles
- Aligns with FATF Recommendations for Financial Institutions
- Consistent with FIU guidance for reporting entities

### 3. Systemic Risk Management
- Commercial banks and high-risk institutions always receive Tier 3
- Protects financial system integrity
- Appropriate oversight for systemically important institutions

### 4. Fairness & Defensibility
- Objective, transparent criteria
- No discretionary overrides
- Documented rationale for each tier assignment
- Consistent application across all institutions

### 5. Supervisory Efficiency
- Bank of Tanzania can focus resources on highest-risk institutions
- Clear expectations for each tier
- Proportionate assessment depth

---

## Implementation in the System

1. **Automatic tier assignment** during introduction phase based on:
   - Selected institution type (category)
   - Reported employee count
   - Reported transaction volume
   - Number of branches
   - Product offerings

2. **Questions filtered dynamically** - users only see questions for their tier

3. **Tier displayed prominently** in assessment reports for transparency

4. **No manual override** - ensures consistency and regulatory defensibility

5. **Supervisors can audit** tier assignments and justifications

## Regulatory Compliance

This tier system ensures compliance with:

✓ **Bank of Tanzania Act** (risk-based supervision)
✓ **Anti-Money Laundering Act (Cap. 423)**
✓ **Banking and Financial Institutions Act**
✓ **FIU Guidelines for Reporting Entities**
✓ **FATF Recommendations 1, 10, 26** (risk-based approach)
✓ **Basel Committee AML/CFT Guidance**

---

This bank-specific tier categorization ensures that Tanzania's financial institutions receive proportionate, risk-based AML/CFT/CPF assessments that protect the financial system while being fair to smaller, lower-risk institutions.
