## 2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal data when you:

- Register your law firm or organisation on the platform
- Use the platform to manage client KYC/CDD, risk assessments, sanctions screening, or related compliance workflows
- Contact us for support or sales enquiries

This Policy applies to **all users** of the platform — firm administrators, management users, staff, lawyers, and compliance officers.

---

## 3. What Personal Data We Collect

### 3.1 Platform Users (Firm Staff)

When a law firm registers and its staff use the platform, we collect:

- Full name, email address, job title, and role within the firm
- Login credentials (passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes; we never store plaintext passwords)
- Login history, device information, and IP addresses for security monitoring
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) enrolment data

### 3.2 Your Clients' Data (KYC/CDD Records)

In performing your AML/CFT obligations, you will enter client personal data into the platform, including:

- Individual clients: full name, date of birth, nationality, identity document numbers, residential address, source of funds and wealth declarations
- Corporate clients: registered name, registration number, beneficial ownership details, directors and authorised signatories
- Transaction monitoring data and risk classifications
- Documents uploaded for KYC/CDD purposes (passports, utility bills, company certificates, etc.)

You, the law firm, are the **Data Controller** for this client data. We act as your **Data Processor** and process it only on your documented instructions.

### 3.3 Usage and Technical Data

- Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
- Pages visited, features used, session duration
- Error logs and performance diagnostics

## 5. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the compliance platform
- Authenticate users and enforce access controls
- Send transactional notifications (password resets, security alerts, billing receipts)
- Monitor for suspicious activity and protect platform integrity
- Comply with our own legal and regulatory obligations
- Respond to support requests

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling for marketing purposes, or sale to third parties.

## 8. Data Retention

| Data Category | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Client KYC/CDD records | 10 years from end of business relationship (AML Act requirement) |
| User account data | Duration of subscription + 2 years |
| Login and security logs | 12 months |
| Billing records | 7 years (tax compliance) |

After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

---

## 9. Security Measures

We implement the following technical and organisational measures:

- AES-256 encryption for sensitive data fields at rest
- TLS 1.2+ encryption for all data in transit
- Role-based access controls (RBAC) with Row Level Security enforced at the database layer
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) available for all user accounts
- Audit logging of all data access and modifications
- Regular security reviews and penetration testing

No system is impenetrable. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected parties within 72 hours of becoming aware, as required by law.

## 10. Your Rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, Cap. 447, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erase** your data (subject to our legal retention obligations)
- **Restrict** processing in certain circumstances
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@iursperitis.co.tz. We will respond within 30 days.
