## Tier Escalation Triggers

**Automatic escalation from Tier 1 to Tier 2 if:**
- Foreign clients present
- PEP exposure identified
- Cross-border structuring
- High-risk sectors served
- Complex ownership structures

**Automatic escalation from Tier 2 to Tier 3 if:**
- Offshore entities served
- Trusts and foundations administered
- Multinational clients served
- Private wealth structuring services

## Risk Ratings

### Inherent Risk
- **Low:** < 1.5
- **Moderate:** 1.5 - 2.49
- **High:** ≥ 2.5

### Compliance
- **Compliant:** ≥ 80%
- **Partially Compliant:** 60-79%
- **Weak:** 40-59%
- **Non-Compliant:** < 40%

### Effectiveness
- **Effective:** ≥ 75%
- **Partially Effective:** 55-74%
- **Weak:** 30-54%
- **Ineffective:** < 30%

### Maturity
- **Optimised:** ≥ 4.5
- **Managed:** 3.5-4.49
- **Defined:** 2.5-3.49
- **Developing:** 1.5-2.49
- **Initial:** < 1.5

### Residual Risk
- **Low:** < 1.5
- **Moderate:** 1.5 - 2.49
- **High:** ≥ 2.5

## Critical Controls (Red Flags if missing)

1. **B1.1** - AML compliance officer
2. **B2.1** - Business-wide risk assessment
3. **B3.1** - CDD procedures
4. **B4.1** - STR procedures
5. **B5.1** - AML training

Missing any critical control triggers automatic "High Risk" residual classification.

1. **Reduced question count:** 117 → 62
2. **Focus on legal services:** Real estate, corporate formation, M&A, trusts
3. **Tanzania-specific:** TLS-aligned, Tanzania legal sector typologies
4. **Simplified tiers:** 3 clear categories (Small, Medium, Large)
5. **Practice area focus:** Legal-specific risks vs. banking products
6. **Professional vulnerabilities:** Legal privilege misuse, client fund management
